Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Who we are

Luna Belly is run by [Business legal name], [Business address, Birmingham, UK]. We are the controller of your personal information. Contact us at bhavikatujela1@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Orders. Your name, email, phone number, delivery address and what you ordered. We need this to fulfil our contract with you and we must keep order records for tax purposes.
  • Chat. The messages you send us and your name if you give it, so we can answer you. This is in our legitimate interest in helping customers.
  • Newsletter. Your email address, with your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by contacting us.
  • Saved baskets. If you enter your email at checkout, or subscribe, we store that address alongside the items in your basket. If you do not finish ordering we send you one reminder, and only one. This is in our legitimate interest in recovering sales. Tell us and we will delete it, and we stop as soon as you place an order.
  • Spam prevention. Your IP address is recorded briefly when you place an order, start a chat or subscribe, so we can block abuse. This is in our legitimate interest in keeping the shop secure.
  • Visit counts. When you view a page we count the page address, the website that linked you here (for example google.com), whether you are on a phone, tablet or computer, and whether you added something to your cart or started checkout. This tells us which pieces people like. We do not use cookies for this, we do not store your IP address, and none of it can identify you. If your browser asks sites not to track you, we do not count your visit. Kept for up to 13 months. This is in our legitimate interest in improving the shop.
  • Error reports. If something on a page fails to work, for example a photo that will not load, your browser sends us the error message, the page address and the type of device, so we can fix it. Kept for 30 days.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores your cart and your chat session on your own device so the site can work. This storage is strictly necessary and is not used to track you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the companies that help us run the shop: our website host (Vercel), our database provider (Supabase), our email provider, the delivery company that brings your parcel, and Google, whose Gemini AI assistant helps us run the shop. When we ask it about orders it sees the order number, what was ordered, your town and your first name with an initial, never your email, phone number or address, and Google does not use this to train its models. Some of these companies process information outside the UK, and where they do, it is protected by safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: up to 6 years, for tax and accounting.
  • Chat messages: up to [12 months].
  • Newsletter email: until you unsubscribe.
  • Saved baskets: up to [30 days], then deleted.
  • Spam-prevention records: normally deleted within a few days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict or stop using it, and ask for it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact us at bhavikatujela1@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
