Waist chains delivered across the Midlands

Wolverhampton

For an anand karaj, a reception or a sangeet, a kamarbandh worn at the natural waist frames the gap between blouse and lehenga. Our Bridal Edit has broader belts and temple pieces that hold their own next to heavy embroidery, and we can help you choose one from a photo of your outfit.

Coventry

Not every waist chain is for a wedding. A single fine strand worn low with jeans, a cropped top or a summer dress is one of the easiest ways to wear one. Everyday Gold collects our lightest, simplest pieces.

Leicester

Leicester's Diwali on Belgrave Road is one of the biggest celebrations of its kind outside India, and festive season is when bells, jhumka drops and coins come out. Have a look at the festive collection, and order early in the season so your piece arrives in good time.

Walsall

Buying for someone else? We can wrap it with tissue, ribbon and a handwritten card, and parcels never contain prices or receipts, so you can send a piece straight to them. See gift ideas or a gift card if you would rather they chose.

West Bromwich and Smethwick

Close enough to Birmingham that your parcel has a very short journey. If you are matching a piece to an outfit bought locally, send us a photo in the chat and we will suggest a few that suit it.

Solihull

Waist chains are made for holidays: over a swimsuit, with linen, or with a sarong. Choose plated pieces for evenings rather than the pool, and read our care guide to keep them looking good for longer.

Dudley

Every chain comes with an extender, and each product page shows the exact waist range it fits. Measure where you want it to sit, not your jeans size. The size guide explains how, in two minutes, and our kamarbandh guide covers how to wear one.

Everywhere else in the UK

We post across the United Kingdom at the same price. For delivery abroad, please ask us in the chat before ordering. Full details are on our delivery and returns page, and you can track your order at any time.
