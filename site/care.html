Jewellery care

Our pieces are plated brass and stainless steel, with freshwater, shell or glass pearls and kundan stones. Some are made by hand and are marked Handmade; the rest come from jewellery makers we buy from. Whichever it is, treated properly it keeps its shine for years. The habits below matter more than any polish.

Every time you wear it

  • Put your jewellery on last, after perfume, hairspray, lotion and sunscreen. Alcohol and oils are what dull plating, not ordinary wear.
  • Take it off before you sleep, shower, swim or exercise.
  • Wipe it with a soft dry cloth when you take it off. That single habit does most of the work.

Storing it

  • Keep each piece in its own cotton pouch so chains do not rub or tangle.
  • Store flat, somewhere dry and out of direct sun. A bathroom shelf is the worst place for it.
  • A silica sachet in the box helps if you live somewhere damp.

Pearls

Pearls are soft and porous. Never soak them, never use jewellery dip or ultrasonic cleaners, and keep them away from vinegar, lemon and household cleaners. Wipe with a barely damp cloth and dry at once.

Kundan and set stones

Kundan settings are held with natural lac and glue. Water loosens them. Clean only the metal around the stones with a dry cloth, and never submerge the piece.

Antique and darkened finishes

Temple and antique-gold pieces are oxidised on purpose, so the shadows in the detail are part of the design. Do not polish them bright, or you will remove the finish. A light buff on the raised areas is enough.

Allergy information

We would rather tell you what these pieces are than make a promise we cannot keep. Every product page lists its base metal and plating.

  • Gold-plated brass. Brass is copper and zinc, with a thin layer of gold-coloured plating over it. Brass darkens naturally, and the plating wears down over years of contact with skin, perfume and friction.
  • Stainless steel. Harder wearing and more stable against moisture, which is why our everyday pieces use it.
  • Pearls. Freshwater, shell or glass, as stated on each product page.
  • Kundan. Glass stones held in place with natural lac, not a hard setting.

We do not claim that these pieces will never tarnish or that nobody will ever react to them. Skin differs, plating thins, and copper in particular bothers some people. If you have a known metal allergy, read the material line on the product page before ordering and ask us in the chat if it is unclear. For a diagnosed allergy, trust your doctor over any product description, including ours.

If the base metal starts to show through and your skin is sensitive, stop wearing the piece and ask us about replating.

How your order arrives

Each piece comes in a cotton pouch inside a rigid box, wrapped in tissue, with a care card. Keep the pouch, as it is the best place to store the piece between wears.
