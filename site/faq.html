Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, open the chat on any shop page and ask us.

How do I know what size to order?

Every chain is adjustable, and each product page gives the exact range it fits, measured around the waist. Our size guide shows you how to measure in under a minute. If you are between sizes, message us in the chat and we will tell you which piece sits best.

What are these made of, and will they irritate my skin?

Each product page lists its base metal and plating. Most pieces are gold-plated brass, which is copper and zinc; a few are stainless steel. Plated finishes wear with time, and plated brass can darken with moisture, perfume and friction. We cannot promise that nobody will react, because skin differs and plating thins with wear. If you have a known metal allergy, read what these pieces are made of first, and ask us in the chat if anything is unclear.

Is your jewellery handmade?

Some of it. Pieces made by hand carry a Handmade badge on their photo and are gathered in our Handmade collection. The rest are chosen from jewellery makers and suppliers in India, Hong Kong and elsewhere. Every product page says what each piece is made of.

Can I wear it every day?

The stainless steel pieces, like the butterfly layered chain, are made for daily wear. The plated brass and kundan pieces are better kept for occasions, as daily friction wears the plating. Each product page lists the material.

How do I pay?

Place your order on the site and we email you to confirm it with payment details. No card details are taken here, and no account is needed. Online card payment is coming soon.

How long does delivery take?

We post from Birmingham and aim to dispatch within [1 to 3] working days of payment clearing. Delivery is charged at the rate shown in your cart and is free over the threshold shown on the site. Full details are on our delivery and returns page.

Can I return something?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to change your mind, and longer if an item is faulty. Our delivery and returns page explains exactly how.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Tick "This is a gift" at checkout to add gift wrapping and a handwritten message card. No parcel ever contains a price or a receipt, so any order is safe to send straight to the recipient. There are also gift cards if you would rather let them choose.

How is my order packaged?

Each piece arrives in a cotton pouch inside a rigid box, wrapped in tissue, with a care card. It is ready to give as a gift without rewrapping.
