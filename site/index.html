Our story

Jewellery that moves with you

The kamarbandh has rested on the waist for centuries, at weddings, at festivals and on ordinary golden evenings. We gather ours from jewellery makers in India, Hong Kong and further afield, choosing each piece for how it sits and how it moves. Some are made by hand, and those carry a Handmade badge so you always know which is which.

Honest materials

Every piece lists its base metal and plating, so you know what you are wearing. What they are made of.

Adjustable fit

Every chain has an extender, so it sits exactly where you like it.

Free shipping

On all orders over £50, carefully boxed.

How do I choose my size?

Measure around your waist where you want the chain to sit. Our chains adjust across a wide range, and you can message us in the chat for help.

How do I care for my chain?

Keep it dry, put it on after perfume and lotion, and store it flat in its pouch.

How do I pay?

Place your order and we will confirm it by email with payment details. Online card payment is coming soon.

Can I return my order?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to change your mind. The details are on our delivery and returns page.

Can I talk to someone?

Yes. Open the chat in the corner of the page and write to us directly.
