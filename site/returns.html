Delivery and returns

Last updated 15 November 2026

Delivery

We send orders from Birmingham to addresses in the United Kingdom. The delivery charge is shown in your cart and at checkout before you place your order, and orders over the amount shown on the site are delivered free.

We aim to send your order within [1 to 3] working days. If we expect a delay we will tell you. If your order has not arrived within 30 days, contact us and you can cancel for a full refund.

For delivery outside the UK, please ask us in the chat before ordering. [Confirm whether you ship abroad]

Changed your mind

You can cancel your order for any reason from the moment you place it until 14 days after the day you receive it.

  1. Tell us you want to cancel, in the chat or by email to [Contact email]. You can use the form below, but any clear message is fine.
  2. Send the item back within 14 days of telling us, unworn and in its original packaging. You pay the return postage, and we recommend a tracked service.
  3. We refund the price of the item and the standard delivery charge you paid, within 14 days of receiving the item back or of you sending us proof of postage.

You may open and inspect a piece as you would in a shop. If it comes back worn or damaged, we may reduce your refund to reflect the loss in value.

Pieces made or altered to your own specification cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

Faulty or wrong items

If your piece arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, contact us with your order number and a photo. Within 30 days of delivery you can choose a full refund. After that we will repair or replace it, or refund you if that is not possible. We pay the return postage for faulty items.
