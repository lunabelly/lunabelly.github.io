Waist chains and kamarbandhs in Birmingham

Luna Belly is a small online shop based in Birmingham. We sell one kind of jewellery: chains for the waist. Kamarbandhs for weddings, belly chains for festivals, and fine everyday chains to wear low on the hips.

For Birmingham weddings and festivals

A kamarbandh is often the last piece chosen for a wedding outfit, and the one that finishes it. Whether your lehenga or saree came from Soho Road, Ladypool Road, Stratford Road or further afield, send us a photo in the chat and tell us where you want the chain to sit. We will suggest pieces that suit the colour and the weight of the embroidery.

For the wedding day itself, look at the Bridal Edit: broader belts, temple work and rows of pearls. For mehndi, sangeet, Diwali, Eid and Vaisakhi, the festive collection has lighter pieces with bells and drops that move when you dance.

Everyday waist chains

Not every piece is for a big occasion. A single fine chain worn low with jeans, or under a loose shirt in summer, is how many of our customers wear them most. Start with Everyday Gold.

Delivery to Birmingham

We post every order from Birmingham. Delivery costs £3.95 and is free on orders over £50, the same anywhere in the UK, from Sutton Coldfield to Selly Oak and across every B postcode. Your parcel comes boxed in a cotton pouch, and if it is a gift, no prices or receipts go inside. Full details are on our delivery and returns page.

Is there a shop I can visit?

Not at the moment. We are an online shop and do not have a walk-in counter. What we do have is a chat on every page. Ask about sizing, a gift, or a piece you cannot decide on.

Getting the size right

Measure around your body where you want the chain to sit. Your jeans size will not tell you, because waist chains usually sit lower or higher than a waistband. Every piece has an extender, and each product page shows the waist range it fits. Our size guide walks you through it. New to waist jewellery? Our kamarbandh guide explains what a kamarbandh is and how to wear one.

Honest about materials

Most of our pieces come from jewellery makers in India, Hong Kong and beyond, and some are handmade, which we always label. Every product page lists the base metal and the plating, and our care guide explains how to keep plated jewellery looking good for longer.

Nearby?
