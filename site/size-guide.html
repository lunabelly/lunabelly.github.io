Size guide

Every Luna Belly chain adjusts, so you do not need an exact size. You do need to know roughly where you want it to sit.

Measure in one minute

  1. Take a soft tape measure, or a piece of string you can lay flat against a ruler afterwards.
  2. Stand relaxed, not holding your stomach in, and breathe normally.
  3. Wrap the tape where you want the chain to rest. For a saree that is usually just below the navel, on the hip bones. For a lehenga it often sits higher, at the natural waist.
  4. Keep the tape snug but not tight. You should fit one finger underneath.
  5. Read the measurement in centimetres. That number is what you compare with the range on each product page.

Where it sits

The same chain looks different depending on where you wear it. Higher on the natural waist gives a more traditional line under a saree pallu. Lower on the hips gives a looser, more modern drape, and you will want a few centimetres more length.

How the adjustment works

Each chain has a hook closure and an extender chain at the back. You shorten or lengthen it by moving the hook along the extender, exactly like a necklace. The product page gives the smallest and largest waist each piece will fit.

Between two pieces

If your measurement falls at the very top of a piece's range, choose a longer one, because a chain pulled to its last link sits stiffly and strains the clasp. If you are near the bottom of the range, the spare extender simply hangs at the back and is hidden by your drape.

Still unsure

Message us in the chat with your measurement and what you plan to wear it with, and we will tell you which piece sits best. It is quicker than guessing and saves a return.
